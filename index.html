Gale Chaser: Storm Match Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Gale Chaser: Storm Match ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android and iOS made by an independent developer (GitHub: oprasky). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases. It plays without an internet connection; when it is online it downloads new levels and fixes.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves your unlocked levels, stars, streak and settings (theme and sound) on your device only. This data never leaves your device through the game. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

If you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account or iCloud), your operating system may include the game's save in that backup. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies, and the developer has no access to it.

Information we collect

None. The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.

When the game starts with an internet connection, it asks Expo's update service (expo.dev, the service the game is built with) whether new levels or fixes are available, downloads them if there are, and reads the current date from the same service so new chapters open on time. These requests carry only what the update needs: the game's version, platform and update channel. Like any internet request they reach the service from your IP address, which Expo handles under its own privacy policy (expo.dev/privacy). No progress, settings or personal information is sent, and the developer receives none.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game asks your phone to show a reminder when a new region opens. These reminders are scheduled on your device by the game itself; no notification service, token or server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Permissions

On Android the game uses permissions only to play sound effects and vibration, and, if you allow it, to show the new-region reminders. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/oprasky/gale-chaser-privacy/issues.
