Gale Chaser: Storm Match Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Gale Chaser: Storm Match ("the game") is an offline puzzle game for Android and iOS made by an independent developer (GitHub: oprasky). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it works without an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves your unlocked levels, stars, streak and settings (theme and sound) on your device only. This data never leaves your device through the game. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

If you have turned on your phone's own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account or iCloud), your operating system may include the game's save in that backup. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies, and the developer has no access to it.

Information we collect

None. The game makes no network requests and contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.

Permissions

On Android the game uses permissions only to play sound effects and vibration. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/oprasky/gale-chaser-privacy/issues.
